19th OpenFOAM Workshop Visualisations

Velocity fields, overset-mesh detail and cylinder-vorticity clips from the 19th OpenFOAM Workshop, presented as lightweight looping videos.

These views show the displayed flow field and mesh behaviour. The clips retain the original GIF frame order and timing; they are qualitative visualisations, not a quantitative validation report.

Clips loop when visible. Use the controls to pause, inspect or enlarge a view. With reduced motion enabled, playback starts only when requested.
